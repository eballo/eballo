1. Technical Contributions
Code Contributions: Record major code changes, features developed, or bugs fixed. Include links to PRs, commits, or tickets.
System Design: Document designs, architectures, or major decisions you contributed to.
Tech Debt: Note any technical debt resolved or refactoring work done.
2. Leadership and Collaboration
Mentorship: Track time spent mentoring junior developers, conducting code reviews, or helping peers.
Cross-Team Collaboration: Document significant interactions with other teams, such as joint projects or knowledge-sharing sessions.
Knowledge Sharing: Record presentations, documentation created, or training sessions led.
3. Problem-Solving
Major Issues Resolved: Note critical incidents or blockers you resolved and their impact on the team or product.
Debugging Challenges: Track notable debugging efforts and solutions implemented.
4. Metrics and Impact
Performance Metrics: Document quantifiable outcomes (e.g., reduced latency by X%, improved build time by Y%).
Business Impact: Highlight contributions that influenced business goals, such as improving user experience or increasing revenue.
5. Process and Efficiency Improvements
Automation: Track workflows or tasks you automated and the time saved.
Process Enhancements: Record contributions to improving team processes, CI/CD pipelines, or documentation.
6. Feedback
Received Feedback: Note feedback from peers, managers, or stakeholders (positive or constructive).
Given Feedback: Track significant feedback you gave to others that helped improve their work.
7. Challenges and Learning
Challenges Overcome: Record significant challenges faced and how you overcame them.
Learning: Keep a log of new skills, technologies, or tools you learned and applied.
8. Communication
Meeting Contributions: Document your role in key meetings (e.g., sprint planning, retrospectives, stakeholder presentations).
Written Communication: Highlight impactful emails, documentation, or proposals.
9. Goals and Progress
Personal Goals: Track progress against your personal development goals.
Team Goals: Record contributions toward team OKRs or goals.
10. Notable Events
Critical Events: Note any high-impact events (e.g., outages, deployments) and your role in them.
Recognition: Record moments of recognition, awards, or praise from others.
